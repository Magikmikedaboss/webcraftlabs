# Welcome to the Archive

The WebCraft Archive is a fictional tradition of inquiry.

Within this collection are recovered investigations, scholarly Treatises, translations, historical reconstructions, and surviving records recovered from multiple civilizations, eras, and intelligences.

No single document represents the complete history.

No single Treatise represents final understanding.

Every publication reflects the best interpretation supported by currently available evidence.

As new evidence is recovered, scholarship evolves.

<BigQuote>

The Archive does not announce truth.

It preserves evidence.

</BigQuote>

<Chapter
number={1}
label="SECTION"
eyebrow="Orientation"
title="How to Read the Archive"
>

There is no required starting point.

Publication order is not historical order.

Recently recovered evidence may revise scholarship published years earlier.

Documents may agree.

Documents may disagree.

Neither guarantees correctness.

Readers are encouraged to compare evidence, evaluate competing interpretations, and construct their own understanding.

Curiosity is part of the Archive.

</Chapter>

<Chapter
number={2}
label="SECTION"
eyebrow="Collections"
title="What You'll Find"
>

The Archive preserves many forms of recovered knowledge.

Investigations present primary evidence.

Treatises summarize current scholarship.

Recovered Records preserve original materials.

Translations reconstruct incomplete or ancient sources.

Scholarly Responses preserve competing interpretations.

Together they form an ongoing record of inquiry rather than a finished history.

</Chapter>

<LabObservation title="Editorial Notice">

Every document represents the best reconstruction available at the time of publication.

Future discoveries may support, revise, or overturn any conclusion presented herein.

Readers are encouraged to consult primary investigations before accepting secondary interpretations.

</LabObservation>
