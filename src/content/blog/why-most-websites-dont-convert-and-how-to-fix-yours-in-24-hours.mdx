## 1. Your Homepage Doesn’t Answer the Only Three Questions Visitors Care About

When someone lands on your site, they’re not admiring your color palette.  
They’re running a mental checklist:

- **What do you do?**
- **Can you help someone like me?**
- **What’s the next step?**

If your homepage doesn’t answer those in the first 3–5 seconds, you lose them.

### ✅ 24-Hour Fix

Rewrite your hero section:

- **Headline:** The outcome you deliver  
  *“We build fast, conversion-ready websites for small businesses.”*
- **Sub-headline:** Proof or specificity  
  *“Trusted by contractors, service pros, and local businesses across the U.S.”*
- **Primary CTA:** One action  
  *“Get a Quote” or “Book a Call”*

No poetry. No vague slogans.  
Just clarity.

---

## 2. Your Website Loads Like It’s Dragging a Boat Anchor

Slow sites feel sketchy.  
People won’t wait for your animations to finish loading — they’ll bounce.

Every extra second of load time kills conversions.

### ✅ 24-Hour Fix

- Compress images (WebP or AVIF)
- Remove unnecessary scripts
- Kill autoplay videos
- Use a lightweight framework or static build
- Move non-critical scripts to the footer

Speed is a **conversion feature**, not a “nice to have.”

## 3. You’re Talking About Yourself Instead of the Problem You Solve

Most business websites read like a résumé:

- “We’ve been in business 10 years…”
- “We pride ourselves on quality…”
- “We offer full-service solutions…”

Visitors care about **their** problem and whether you can fix it.

### ✅ 24-Hour Fix

Rewrite your copy using this formula:

**Problem → Solution → Proof → Next Step**

Example:

**Problem:** “Your website looks fine but isn’t generating leads.”  
**Solution:** “We rebuild sites for speed, clarity, and conversion.”  
**Proof:** “Clients see 2–5x more quote requests within 30 days.”  
**Next Step:** “Book a free 15-minute call.”

---

## 4. You Have Zero Trust Signals (Or They’re Buried)

People don’t convert when they feel uncertain.

Trust is built through:

- Testimonials with real names
- Before/after screenshots
- Case studies
- Photos of real work
- Clear pricing ranges
- A simple explanation of your process

### ✅ 24-Hour Fix

Add a **Why People Trust Us** section above the fold or right below it.

Make it scannable.  
Make it real.  
Make it impossible to miss.

---

## 5. Your CTA Is Weak, Confusing, or Non-Existent

A CTA should feel like the **natural next step**, not a surprise.

Bad CTAs:

- “Learn More”
- “Contact Us”
- “Submit”

Good CTAs:

- “Get a Quote”
- “Book a Call”
- “See Pricing”
- “Start Your Project”

### ✅ 24-Hour Fix

Pick **one** primary CTA and repeat it across the page.

People convert when the path is obvious.

---

## 6. Your Mobile Experience Is an Afterthought

Most of your traffic is on mobile.  
Most mobile visitors are scanning, not reading.

If your site feels cramped, slow, or confusing on a phone, you’re bleeding money.

### ✅ 24-Hour Fix

- Increase font sizes
- Add spacing
- Reduce text blocks
- Make CTAs thumb-friendly
- Remove clutter
- Test on an actual phone (not just a browser preview)

---

## 7. Your Website Has No “Conversion Path”

A lot of sites are just… pages.  
No flow.  
No narrative.  
No guided path from curiosity → trust → action.

### ✅ 24-Hour Fix

Rebuild your homepage sections in this order:

1. **Outcome-driven hero**
2. **Proof you’re legit**
3. **What you do + who it’s for**
4. **Your process (simple, 3 steps)**
5. **Social proof**
6. **CTA that feels inevitable**

This structure alone can double conversions.
